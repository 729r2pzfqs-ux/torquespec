1-3/8" torque in practice

A dry 1-3/8" Grade 5 bolt takes 1469.0 ft·lbs (1991.7 N·m) on the coarse 6 TPI thread. Oiled, that falls to 1101.8 ft·lbs; the dry figure on an oiled bolt overshoots the intended clamp load by about 33%. Grade 8 needs 2382.2 ft·lbs (62% more), and the fine 12 TPI thread raises the Grade 5 figure to 1672.5 ft·lbs.

1-3/8" bolts carry torques beyond most hand wrenches; a torque multiplier or hydraulic wrench is normal, and critical joints are better controlled by bolt tension than by torque. Grade 5 uses the reduced 74 ksi proof stress above 1" and Grade 2 the 33 ksi figure, which is why Grade 8 pulls further ahead at these sizes.
