9/16" torque in practice

A dry 9/16" Grade 5 bolt takes 108.8 ft·lbs (147.5 N·m) on the coarse 12 TPI thread. Oiled, that falls to 81.6 ft·lbs; the dry figure on an oiled bolt overshoots the intended clamp load by about 33%. Grade 8 needs 153.6 ft·lbs (41% more), and the fine 18 TPI thread raises the Grade 5 figure to 121.3 ft·lbs.

At 9/16", torques are into 1/2" drive and 3/4" drive territory. SAE J429 drops the Grade 2 proof stress from 55 to 33 ksi above 3/4", and Grade 5 from 85 to 74 ksi above 1", so the grades do not scale evenly through this range. Structural connections at this diameter are normally ASTM F3125 (A325/A490) bolts installed by turn-of-nut rather than by torque.
