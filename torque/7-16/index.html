7/16" torque in practice

A dry 7/16" Grade 5 bolt takes 49.4 ft·lbs (67.0 N·m) on the coarse 14 TPI thread. Oiled, that falls to 37.1 ft·lbs; the dry figure on an oiled bolt overshoots the intended clamp load by about 33%. Grade 8 needs 69.8 ft·lbs (41% more), and the fine 20 TPI thread raises the Grade 5 figure to 55.2 ft·lbs.

7/16" bolts are everywhere in automotive and equipment work. Grade 5 is the usual choice; Grade 8 goes on suspension, driveline and anything that sees shock load. Oiling the threads cuts the required torque by a quarter, not far short of the difference between Grade 5 and Grade 8, so knowing whether the threads are lubricated matters as much as knowing the grade.
