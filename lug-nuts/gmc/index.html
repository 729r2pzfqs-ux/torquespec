How GMC Wheel Torque Works

GMC shares GM hardware with Chevrolet: full-size Sierra and Yukon take 140 ft·lbs on 6-lug M14×1.5 studs (22 mm socket), while the Acadia and Terrain crossovers take 100 ft·lbs (19 mm socket).

Tightening Any GMC Wheel

Star sequence, always. Never work around the wheel in a circle. Five-lug wheels go 1 → 3 → 5 → 2 → 4; six-lug wheels go 1 → 4 → 2 → 5 → 3 → 6; eight-lug wheels go 1 → 5 → 3 → 7 → 2 → 6 → 4 → 8.

Two passes. Snug everything to about half spec first, then bring each lug nut to full torque in the same order.

Dry threads. GMC publishes these figures for clean, dry threads. Lubricant at the same wrench setting produces far more clamp load than intended.

Re-torque after 50–100 miles. This matters most on alloy wheels and after any wheel-off service.
